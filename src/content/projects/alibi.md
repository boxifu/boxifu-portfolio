## Overview

Alibi is a narrative puzzle game and USC Games MFA thesis project developed in collaboration between USC Games, Berklee College of Music, and Otis College of Art and Design. The player is a therapist in training returning to their hometown to investigate the disappearance of a childhood friend.

The old portfolio describes Alibi as a personal investigation into a mystery that began ten years earlier. The tone is reflective and tense, combining school setting exploration, dialogue, evidence, and small interactive scenes.

## Contributions

As a programmer, I implemented:

- A dialogue system and custom text bubble UI using Unity and Pixel Crushers.
- An interactable blackboard mini game that preserves drawing in the environment.
- Inventory and item pickup interactions.
- A broom fighting sequence with character knockback, trigger-area detection, and timer systems.

As a general artist, I created:

- 3D environment art support and shadow movement simulation.
- Character and UI animation implementation.
- Bathroom scene art and drawing implementation.
- Environment audio design.
- Main menu UI and color transition effects.

## Content Note

Alibi includes depictions of bullying, psychological trauma, and systemic violence within a school setting.
