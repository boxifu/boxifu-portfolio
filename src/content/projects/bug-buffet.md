## Overview

Bug Buffet is a modification of the classic Up the River board game. Four players act as bugs trying to reach sugar cubes before they are washed away by sink water.

## Contributions

- Contributed movable tile and multi-grid tile concepts.
- Helped design narrative context, dice rules, player actions, and tile replacement rules.
- Created card art assets, grid designs, edge designs, special tile designs, and rulebook diagrams.
