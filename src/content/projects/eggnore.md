## Overview

Egregore is a first-person adventure game where the player uncovers an occult conspiracy in modern Cairo. The game centers on a graphing mechanic that lets players connect clues, reason through uncertain information, and decide what counts as truth.

The project was developed as a 2023-2024 AGP/MFA thesis collaboration between USC Games, Berklee College of Music, and Otis College of Art and Design. Its inspirations include NORCO, Riven, and The Case of the Golden Idol, with the final game built in Unreal Engine 5.

## Contributions

As an environment artist, I worked on:

- Designing the main spaces, including a modern Cairo street and a Middle Eastern cafe.
- Creating dynamically changing skyboxes.
- Editing existing 2D and 3D assets so each room fit the project direction.
- Using lighting as an attention-guidance tool to direct players toward points of interest.

As a technical artist, I worked on:

- Camera post-processing for a vibrant but realistic visual tone.
- Particle effects for the graph and connection UI.
- Depth of field, fog, and volumetric lighting.
- Per-object outline and glow shader effects.
- 2D marketing art assets for the Steam page and project website.
