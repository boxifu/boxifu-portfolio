## Overview

Life Survival Guide is a cooperative card-discarding game designed to evoke compassion. Three players can try to handle their own challenges independently, but the easier path is to communicate, collaborate, and win together.

## Contributions

- Created box cover and rulebook diagrams.
- Created problem cards and solution cards.
- Designed mechanics to evoke emotion without explicitly naming the target emotion.
- Worked on game flow, win condition, replayability, and overall play experience.
