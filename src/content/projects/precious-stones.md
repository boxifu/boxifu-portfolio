## Overview

Precious Stones is a character action-adventure game made for the Spring 2023 iteration of CTIN 532 at USC Games. The player controls a dwarf who follows his father's wishes, explores environments, uses a hammer, fights monsters, and solves interaction-based puzzles.

## Contributions

- Implemented collectible and interaction mechanics, including door locks, crystal responses, and skill acquisition.
- Built player actions and movement systems.
- Implemented animation behavior for the main character, enemies, environment objects, and puzzle mechanisms.
- Created the main character 3D model, rigging, skinning, and related technical art support.
- Worked on SFX and background music management.
