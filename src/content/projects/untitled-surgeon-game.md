## Overview

Unnamed Surgeon Game was made for the 2024 Global Game Jam at USC Games under the theme "Make Me Laugh." The player acts as an intern nurse who must find surgical instruments while keeping enough light on the surgeon and operating table.

## Contributions

- Implemented cabinet and closet opening mechanisms.
- Built character and interactable animation systems.
- Implemented tutorial and UI behavior.
- Created environment layout, lighting, and post-processing for a horror-comedy feeling.
- Organized room 3D models, materials, and textures for the scene.
