## Overview

Unnamed Surgeon Game was made for the 2024 Global Game Jam at USC Games under the theme "Make Me Laugh." The player acts as a surgeon who needs to organize the operating room before the lights run out.

The central loop asks the player to find surgical instruments while keeping enough light on the surgeon and operating table. When the lights begin to fade, the player can make funny noises with items to bring the light back up.

## Contributions

As an engineer, I implemented:

- Cabinet and closet opening mechanisms.
- Character and interactable animation systems.
- Tutorial and UI behavior.

As an artist and technical artist, I created:

- Environment layout, lighting, and post-processing for a horror-comedy feeling.
- 3D model texturing, material work, and scene organization.
- Title/menu presentation and related visual polish.

## Development Notes

The project was built quickly for a jam setting, so the design focuses on a readable room objective, simple object interactions, and an immediate comedy-horror lighting loop.
