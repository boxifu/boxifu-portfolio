## Overview

Wedding Royale is a 1v1v1v1 area-control game under a romance theme. Four players each control a bride-and-groom pair and compete for wedding resources.

## Contributions

- Contributed map design using basic graph theory.
- Worked on fighting deck design and production.
- Helped design the board theme and background story.
- Supported design photo journals, meeting documentation, playtest notes, main loop design, player actions, and win conditions.
